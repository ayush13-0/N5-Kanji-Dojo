# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in N5 Kanji Dojo welcoming, respectful and constructive for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus on the issue, not the person.
- Respect different experience levels and language backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to project spaces, issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-related interactions.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact route on the project's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly as possible.
